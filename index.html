Questions, answered.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Every lead we generate is 100% exclusive to you. We build the campaigns under your own brand and send each lead to you alone. No shared lists, no bidding against five other contractors.

What does it cost?

Managed lead generation, where we run appointment setting, email outreach or LinkedIn ads for you, starts from R19,950 per month (August 2026) — the full breakdown is on the pricing page. Exclusive lead campaigns, websites, SEO and ad work are scoped on the call. No lock-in contracts.

How fast is the website live?

Your site is live in 2 to 3 weeks from the first call. You get daily progress updates while we build, so you always know where things stand.

Do I pay upfront?

No upfront payments on websites, SEO or ad campaigns. If you are not happy, you do not pay. Managed lead generation retainers are the exception: those are prepaid monthly, because the data and the ad spend start on day one. Rates are on the pricing page.

Which trades do you work with?

Roofers, plumbers, HVAC, electrical, solar, landscaping and other home service trades across the US. If your customers search online before they hire, we can help.

What happens on the call?

You get 30 minutes with Bradley. He maps your market, your goals and where jobs are leaking, then tells you what we would do and what it costs. Free, direct, no pitch deck.
