Lead generation

Leads for electricians in South Africa, sent to you alone.

We generate leads for electricians in South Africa under your own brand: homeowners with a tripping DB board, a certificate of compliance to sort out before a sale, or backup power to put in. Each one is checked before it reaches you, and nobody else gets the same enquiry.

Leads by trade: Roofers · Plumbers · Electricians · All trades

The electrical jobs we aim the campaigns at

Electrical work runs from a quick fault to a full installation, and the leads should match the work you want more of. We agree that with you before anything runs. The usual ones:

  • Fault finding: tripping circuits, dead plugs, part of the house with no power
  • Certificates of compliance (CoC) for a house sale
  • Inverters, batteries and backup power
  • DB board upgrades and rewiring
  • Lights, plugs and outdoor lighting
  • Solar PV, if it’s part of what you do

CoC and backup power enquiries work differently

A CoC enquiry usually comes from a house sale, so it arrives with a transfer date attached and the homeowner wants a date from you, not a promise to call back. Answer those the same day and give a time.

Backup power is the other way round: bigger jobs, quoted slowly, with the homeowner comparing two or three electricians. If you install inverters and batteries, tell us, because those campaigns are worth turning up when load shedding is back in the news and down when it isn’t.

What an electrician lead from us is

An enquiry from a homeowner who contacted your business by name. The campaign runs under your brand, so it isn’t passed around a list of electricians. Before it reaches your phone or inbox we check the contact details, the job they’re asking about (a fault, a CoC or an installation) and whether the address is in your area. Then it lands in real time.

It’s an enquiry to work, not a job already sold. Electricians who call back fast and follow up get work out of them. If you’d rather have customers ringing you than enquiries to call back, we’ll tell you on the call whether ads or SEO on your own site would suit you better.

Shared electrician leads or exclusive?

Shared leads are cheaper because the same homeowner is sold to several electricians at once, and the job usually goes to whoever rings first. Exclusive leads cost more per lead and let you quote on the job rather than on speed alone. Exclusive vs shared leads works through the sums.

What electrician lead generation costs

The campaigns are scoped on the call rather than sold off a rate card, because the cost depends on which jobs you want, your area and how many leads your team can get to. The monthly packages on our pricing page are built for businesses that sell to other businesses, so as an electrician you get your figure on the call. There’s no lock-in contract.

Questions electricians ask us

Are the leads shared with other electricians?

No. Each lead goes to you alone and is never resold. The homeowner enquired with your business, because the campaign runs under your name.

Can you send only CoC work, or only installations?

We scope the campaigns to the jobs and areas you agree on the call. A homeowner with a tripping board doesn’t always know what the fault is, so some enquiries need a short call to find out what the job really is.

How soon do the first leads come in?

There’s a build month first: the campaigns, the tracking and your approvals. So the first enquiries land in month two at the earliest, then they reach you in real time.

What if a campaign isn’t working?

Say so. If a campaign isn’t producing leads worth having, we rebuild it at no charge.
