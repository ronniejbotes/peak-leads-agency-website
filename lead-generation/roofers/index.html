Lead generation

Leads for roofers in South Africa, sent to you alone.

We generate roofing leads in South Africa under your own brand: homeowners asking about a leak, waterproofing or a new roof, checked before they reach you. Nobody else gets the same enquiry, so you aren’t racing four other roofers to the same house.

Leads by trade: Roofers · Plumbers · Electricians · All trades

What a roofing lead from us is

A homeowner who asked your business, by name, about their roof. The campaign runs under your brand, so the enquiry comes to you directly, not through a directory that sells it on. Before it reaches your phone or inbox we check the contact details, the job they’re actually asking about and whether the house is inside the area you work. Then it lands in real time, not in a weekly export.

It’s an enquiry to work, not a job already sold. Roofers who call back fast and follow up get work out of these. If you’d rather have customers ringing you than enquiries to call back, ads or SEO on your own site may suit you better, and we’ll tell you that on the call.

The roofing jobs we aim the campaigns at

A leak repair and a full re-roof come from different homeowners and are worth very different money, so we agree the job types with you before anything runs. The usual ones:

  • Leak repairs, often the week after the first heavy rain
  • Waterproofing on flat roofs and parapet walls
  • Gutters, flashings and downpipes
  • Repairs after storm damage
  • Full re-roofs and new roofs
  • Roof painting and coating

Tell us the jobs you’d rather not take, too. An enquiry for work you don’t want still costs you time on the phone.

Why the callback matters more than the lead

Leak enquiries follow the rain, so the busy months in the winter-rainfall Cape aren’t the same as the summer storm season inland. When it pours, homeowners tend to hire whoever calls first. An exclusive lead means no other roofer is sent the same enquiry. It doesn’t make the homeowner wait for you until the evening.

So before you buy leads from us or anyone else, decide who calls back, and how fast. If nobody in your business can get back to an enquiry within the hour, fix that first.

Shared roofing leads or exclusive?

Shared roofing leads are cheaper because the same homeowner is sold to several roofers, often within minutes. Exclusive leads cost more per lead and usually work out cheaper per booked job, if you phone fast. Exclusive vs shared leads has the sums, and what roofing leads cost in South Africa compares every source side by side, including running your own Google and Meta ads.

What roofing lead generation costs

Exclusive campaigns for a roofing business are scoped on the call rather than sold off a rate card, because the cost depends on the areas you cover and how many leads you can realistically work in a week. The monthly packages on our pricing page are built for businesses that sell to other businesses, so as a roofer you get your figure on the call. There’s no lock-in contract.

What helps on your side

  • Someone who answers the phone or WhatsApp in working hours and calls back within minutes
  • Photos of roofs you’ve finished yourself, for the pages and the ads
  • The suburbs and towns you’ll travel to, and the ones you won’t
  • A page that loads fast on a phone with your number on it. If yours doesn’t, the website usually gets scoped with the campaign
  • A Google Business Profile with recent reviews, because a homeowner who gets your call will often look you up first

Questions roofers ask us

Will other roofers get the same lead?

No. Each lead goes to you alone and is never resold. The campaign runs under your brand, so the homeowner enquired with your business in the first place.

Can you send only re-roofs, or only repairs?

We scope the campaigns to the jobs and areas you agree on the call. Homeowners don’t always describe a roof the way a roofer would, though, so some enquiries need a quick call to find out what the job really is.

How soon do the first leads come in?

Getting a campaign live takes a build month: the campaigns, the tracking and your approvals. So the first enquiries land in month two at the earliest, and after that they reach you in real time.

What if the leads aren’t any good?

Tell us. If a campaign isn’t producing leads worth having, we rebuild it at no charge.
