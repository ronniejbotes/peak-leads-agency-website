Lead generation

Leads for plumbers in South Africa, sent to you alone.

We generate leads for plumbers in South Africa under your own brand: homeowners with a burst geyser, a blocked drain or a bathroom to redo, checked before they reach you. Nobody else gets the same enquiry, so you aren’t one of five plumbers phoning the same house.

Leads by trade: Roofers · Plumbers · Electricians · All trades

Emergencies and planned work are different leads

A geyser that burst at six in the morning and a bathroom renovation next month aren’t the same enquiry. The first goes to the plumber who answers. The second goes to the one who sends a clear quote with photos of similar work. We agree on the call which of the two you want more of, and set the campaigns up for that.

The plumbing jobs we usually aim at:

  • Burst and leaking geysers, and geyser replacements
  • Blocked drains and toilets
  • Leaks, including the ones nobody can find
  • Plumbing for bathroom and kitchen renovations
  • Taps, toilets and showers that need replacing

If you only want call-outs inside your own radius, or nothing after hours, say so. Enquiries you can’t take still eat your time, so we’d rather not bring them in.

What a plumbing lead from us is

A homeowner who contacted your business, by name. The campaign runs under your brand, so they aren’t filling in a form on a quote site that sends the job to several plumbers on its list. Before the lead reaches your phone or inbox we check the contact details, the job they’re actually asking about, and whether the address is in your area. Then it arrives in real time.

It’s an enquiry to work, not a job already booked. Plumbers who reply fast and follow up turn these into work. If you’d rather have customers ringing you than enquiries to call back, we’ll say on the call whether ads or SEO on your own site would suit you better.

Speed decides most plumbing jobs

With water on the floor, nobody waits until tomorrow for a callback. If the leads come in while you’re up in someone’s roof, someone else needs to pick them up: a partner, the office, or at least a WhatsApp reply saying when you’ll ring. Exclusive leads mean no other plumber is sent the same enquiry. They don’t make the homeowner more patient.

Already running Google Ads and the phone isn’t ringing? Google Ads getting clicks but no calls goes through the checks in order, from whether the calls are being counted to whether anyone answers.

Shared plumbing leads or exclusive?

Quote-request sites often sell one plumbing job to several plumbers. That can pay if you’re the fastest on the phone every single time. If you’d rather quote on the job than on the race, exclusive leads under your own name are the other route. Exclusive vs shared leads shows the arithmetic with round numbers.

What plumbing lead generation costs

For a plumbing business the campaigns are scoped on the call rather than sold off a rate card, because the cost depends on your area and how many enquiries you and your team can get to in a day. The monthly packages on our pricing page are built for businesses that sell to other businesses, so as a plumber you get your figure on the call. There’s no lock-in contract.

Questions plumbers ask us

Are the leads shared with other plumbers?

No. Each one goes to you alone and is never resold. The homeowner enquired with your business, because the campaign carries your name.

Can the campaigns cover only my suburbs?

Yes. The campaigns are scoped to the areas you actually work, agreed on the call, and we check each lead’s address against them before it reaches you.

Do I need a website for this?

Usually, yes. Every lead lands on a page, and a slow one loses them. If yours is slow on a phone or hides your number, the website usually gets scoped with the campaign.

How soon would leads start?

A campaign needs a build month first: writing it, wiring up the tracking and getting your approval. So the first enquiries land in month two at the earliest. From then on they come through in real time.
