Common questions

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each lead goes to you and only you. Because the campaigns run under your own brand, the homeowner is enquiring with your business directly, not with a directory that then sells the same enquiry on to four of your competitors.

What does exclusive lead generation cost?

It depends on your trade, the areas you cover and how many leads you can realistically work, so it is scoped on the call rather than sold off a rate card. Managed lead generation runs on fixed monthly packages, set out in full on the pricing page.

How are the leads verified?

Every lead is checked and filtered before it reaches you: the contact details, the job the person is actually asking about, and whether they sit inside the area you work.

How quickly do leads start arriving?

Once a campaign is live, leads reach you in real time. Getting there takes a build month: writing the campaigns, wiring up the tracking and getting your approvals before anything goes out. On the managed packages that means the first enquiries land in month two.
