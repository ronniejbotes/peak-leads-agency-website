Signs your leads are costing you more than they bring in

If you already buy leads, or have tried, these are the usual warning signs:

  • You are one of four or five businesses phoning the same homeowner, and the job goes to whoever rang first.
  • You quote low because you know three other quotes are already on the table.
  • Enquiries reach you hours after the person asked, and by then they have booked someone else.
  • Many of them are outside your area, or for work you don't do.
  • The quiet weeks come back the moment you stop paying.

Exclusive leads are aimed at the race, the low quotes and the late enquiries: nobody else is sent the enquiry, and it reaches you in real time. Scoping the campaigns to your jobs and your areas is how we cut down the out-of-area ones. The quiet weeks are where SEO comes in.

What is the difference between exclusive and shared leads?

A shared lead is sold to several businesses at once, so the homeowner hears from all of them and the job usually goes to whoever rings first. An exclusive lead is generated under your brand and goes to you alone. Both have a place, and we go through the trade-off in full in exclusive versus shared leads. Pay per lead, shared leads or a campaign under your name?

There are three common ways to buy leads, and they suit different businesses.

  • Pay per lead. A lead seller charges a set fee for each enquiry it sends you. It feels safe, because you only pay when something arrives. Before you sign, ask what counts as a lead, whether the same person is sold to anyone else, and what happens when the number is wrong.
  • Shared leads. A quote site sends one enquiry to several businesses. Each lead costs less, and you are in a race to phone first.
  • A campaign under your own name. The ads and pages carry your brand, the homeowner enquires with you, and nobody else is sent the lead. The customer is yours from the first contact, not the seller's.

Why am I getting leads but not winning the jobs?

Often it is one of three things. The lead was shared and someone else phoned first. The callback came too late. Or the enquiry was for work or an area you don't cover. An exclusive lead rules out the first, because nobody else is sent it. The second comes down to calling back fast, within the hour. Scoping the campaigns to your jobs and areas, and checking each lead's address before it reaches you, is how we cut down the third.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each lead goes to you and only you. Because the campaigns run under your own brand, the homeowner is enquiring with your business directly, not with a directory that then sells the same enquiry on to four of your competitors.
