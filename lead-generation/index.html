Who is exclusive lead generation for?

Roofers, plumbers, electricians, solar and aircon installers, borehole and water specialists and builders who can answer the phone and have capacity to fill. If nobody in your business can get back to an enquiry within the hour, fix that first, because exclusivity only pays if you call.

What is the difference between exclusive and shared leads?

A shared lead is sold to several businesses at once, so the homeowner hears from all of them and the job usually goes to whoever rings first. An exclusive lead is generated under your brand and goes to you alone. Both have a place, and we go through the trade-off in full in exclusive versus shared leads.

How exclusive leads and shared leads differ in practice
What is being compared Exclusive leads Shared leads
Who else gets the same enquiry Nobody Several businesses, often at the same moment
Whose name the homeowner sees Yours, because the campaign runs under your brand The directory's or the vendor's
What decides who wins the job Whether you call back and how you handle the job Largely who phones first
Price per lead Higher, because you are not sharing it Lower per lead
What it does to your quoting You quote the job on its merits Pressure to undercut the other three quotes
Who the customer ends up belonging to You, with your brand on the enquiry from the start The vendor, who can sell you the same customer again

What else do people ask before buying leads?

Four questions come up on nearly every call: whether the leads really are exclusive, what they cost, how they are checked before you see them, and how soon the first ones arrive. Straight answers to each are below.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each lead goes to you and only you. Because the campaigns run under your own brand, the homeowner is enquiring with your business directly, not with a directory that then sells the same enquiry on to four of your competitors.

What does exclusive lead generation cost?

It depends on your trade, the areas you cover and how many leads you can realistically work, so it is scoped on the call rather than sold off a rate card. Managed lead generation runs on fixed monthly packages, set out in full on the pricing page.

How are the leads verified?

Every lead is checked and filtered before it reaches you: the contact details, the job the person is actually asking about, and whether they sit inside the area you work.

How quickly do leads start arriving?

Once a campaign is live, leads reach you in real time. Getting there takes a build month: writing the campaigns, wiring up the tracking and getting your approvals before anything goes out. On the managed packages that means the first enquiries land in month two.
