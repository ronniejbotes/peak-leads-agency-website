What we do.

AI SEO

AI SEO, also called GEO, is the work of getting your business named when a search engine writes the answer instead of listing links. We structure your pages so a clean answer can be lifted out of them, and make sure every other mention of your business online agrees with it. Nobody can guarantee you a spot in an AI answer, and we don't pretend to.
