What do you get for a monthly SEO fee?

  • Google Business Profile work aimed at Maps and the local pack: categories, services, photos, posts and the details Google actually reads
  • Technical SEO: page speed, site structure, internal links and schema, fixed rather than listed in a report

Who is SEO worth paying for?

Established South African businesses that want calls which do not stop the moment the ad budget does. SEO is the slow lever: it builds over months rather than weeks, and it works hardest on a site built to convert the traffic it earns. If your customers search before they hire, there is a position here worth owning.

SEO or paid ads: which should you start with?

Most businesses end up running both, because they do different jobs. Paid ads buy attention for as long as the card is being charged. SEO earns it and keeps earning it after the spend stops. If you need work booked this month, start with ads. If you are tired of renting your enquiries, start with SEO.

What else do people ask before hiring an SEO agency?

Five questions come up on nearly every call: what actually lands in the inbox each month, how long the work takes to show, what AI search changes, whether the website has to be rebuilt first, and what it costs. Straight answers to each are below.

What do I actually get sent each month?

A plain-English report on rankings, traffic and calls, with the work we did that month listed against it. No lock-in contract sits behind it, so if the report stops justifying the fee you stop paying it.

How long does SEO take to work?

Longer than paid ads. Technical fixes and Google Business Profile work can shift things reasonably early. Rankings for the competitive service terms build over months, not weeks. If you need jobs booked this month, run paid ads alongside the SEO and let the rankings compound in the background.

What about AI search and AI Overviews?

Google AI Mode has been live in South Africa since August 2025, so some of your customers are already getting a written answer instead of ten links. The foundations are the same ones classic SEO needs, which is why we do not sell it as a separate product. The extra work is structuring pages so an answer can be lifted cleanly and making sure your business is described identically everywhere online. It is set out in full on the AI SEO page.

Do I need a new website first?

Not always. If your current site is fast enough and structured sensibly, we work with it. If it is slow, dated or built on a platform that fights back, rebuilding it first is usually cheaper than paying us to drag it up the rankings.

What does SEO cost?

It is scoped on the call, because a single-suburb plumber and a national installer need very different amounts of work. We do not sell SEO off a shelf. The managed packages we do publish, and what each one includes, are set out on the pricing page.
