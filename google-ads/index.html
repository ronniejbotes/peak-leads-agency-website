Common questions

Do you run Local Services Ads?

No. Google runs Local Services Ads and the Google Guaranteed badge in a short list of countries, and South Africa is not one of them, so anyone offering them here is selling something they cannot deliver. The same job gets done locally with search ads, a properly maintained Google Business Profile and a steady flow of reviews, which is what we set up instead.

What should my ad budget be?

It depends on your trade, your service area and what a single job is worth to you. We work that out on the call rather than guessing on a web page. Some of the monthly packages carry ad management already, and anything outside them gets scoped on the call.

Google Ads or Meta ads?

Usually both, in different roles. Google Ads catches people who are already searching for what you do. Meta reaches people before they start looking, which is what fills a quiet week. Most accounts run both, weighted to whichever is cheaper per booked job that month.

How often do you touch the account?

Weekly. Search terms get reviewed and the wasteful ones cut, bids and budgets get moved toward whatever is booking jobs, and you get told what changed. An account nobody opens for a month is where the budget quietly leaks.
