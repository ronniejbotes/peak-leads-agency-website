Our story

Built on trust.

Peak Leads is a small South African team that builds websites, SEO and exclusive lead systems for home service businesses. Six people: a founder, two developers, an SEO specialist, a social media manager and an operations manager. You work with the people who do the work, not an account manager who forwards your email.

Peak Leads started with one mission: help trades businesses win online without paying agency prices for agency overhead. What began as one founder taking on roofing and plumbing clients has grown into a full team that competes with far bigger firms, on the only measure that counts — the number of real enquiries your phone rings with.

We do not just build websites. We build the thing behind the website: the search visibility that gets you found, the pages that answer what a buyer actually types, and the follow-up that turns a form fill into a booked job.

Teamwork makes the dream work.

Bradley Hartmann, Founder

Why does a South African team build for American trades?

Because the time zones work in your favour. While your market sleeps, we are already working. You wake up to progress, not excuses — and the daily update is sitting in your inbox before your first job of the morning.

The team is based in South Africa and builds for roofers, plumbers, HVAC crews and contractors across the United States. People who do real work and deserve marketing that works as hard as they do. Rates go further, standards do not drop, and you get a full team for what a single local hire would cost.

What does the guarantee actually say?

No upfront payment on websites, SEO or ad campaigns: if you are not happy, you do not pay. Managed lead generation retainers are the one exception, because the data and the ad spend start on day one, so those are prepaid monthly.

We would rather tell you that here than bury it in the small print. The same goes for the answer we give when a piece of work is not worth doing — you will hear “you do not need this” from us before you hear it from your accountant. Our pricing is published, so you can check the numbers before you ever get on a call.

How fast does a site go live?

Two to three weeks from the first call, in most cases. That is not a stretch target — it is what a fixed process with six specialists and no committee produces. Design, build and SEO run in parallel rather than in a relay, and Jesse keeps the hand-offs from stalling.

The numbers so far

A 4.9 out of 5 average rating across 70+ client reviews. More than R114M in client revenue generated. Sites live in 2 to 3 weeks from the first call. The work is quiet. The results are not.
