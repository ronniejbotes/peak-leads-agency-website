About

The person behind Peak Leads.

Peak Leads is not a big agency. It is one founder, a tight system and a short list of clients who get his full attention. This page is the honest version of who you would be working with.

From South Africa, for American trades.

I'm Bradley Hart. I grew up in South Africa, and I build websites, SEO and lead systems for home service businesses across the United States. Roofers, plumbers, HVAC crews, contractors. People who do real work and deserve marketing that works as hard as they do.

The time zones are a quiet advantage. While your market sleeps, I am already working. You wake up to progress updates, not excuses.

Locked in.

My routine is simple. Train early. Work in long, focused blocks. Ship something every day. Send every client an update before the day ends. Discipline is not a word I put on a poster. It is the reason a site goes live in 2 to 3 weeks instead of 3 months.

Outside client work, I coach a small group of young builders through the same systems Peak Leads runs every day. Teaching it keeps me sharp.

Faith and family.

My faith keeps me honest. My family keeps me grounded. Both shape how I run this business: tell the truth, do the work, and treat every client the way I would want to be treated. That is why the guarantee stays simple. No upfront payments. If you are not happy, you do not pay.

The numbers so far.

A 4.9 out of 5 average rating across 70+ client reviews. More than $7M in client revenue generated. Sites live in 2 to 3 weeks from the first call. The work is quiet. The results are not.
